# Tunday Kababi

## Narration
No visit to Lucknow is complete without tasting the Galouti Kebab at Tunday Kababi. These kebabs are famously so soft that they melt in your mouth. The shop was founded in 1905 by Haji Murad Ali, and the word tunda refers to the one-armed cook who made them. Legend says the recipe was created for a Nawab who could no longer chew hard food, so the meat is minced fine and cooked with a secret blend of spices. Enjoy them with ulte tawe ka paratha, and go with an empty stomach!

## Facts
- Tunday Kababi was founded in 1905 by Haji Murad Ali, in Chowk, Lucknow. The Aminabad branch is the best known.
- Its famous dish is the Galouti Kebab: very soft, finely minced meat patties cooked on a flat griddle.
- Legend: Galouti kebabs were created for an elderly Nawab with no teeth, who still wanted kebabs. This is a popular story, not a documented fact.
- The word tunda means one-armed; the founder is said to have had a disabled arm.
- The spice blend is a closely guarded family secret, said to contain over a hundred ingredients.
- The meat is traditionally buffalo or mutton. It is non-vegetarian, so vegetarians should ask for the vegetarian alternatives.
- Served with ulte tawe ka paratha, a flaky flatbread cooked on the back of the griddle.
- Visiting tip: go at lunch or early evening, expect a queue, and the food is mild rather than very spicy.
